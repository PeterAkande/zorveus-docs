Choose how your application uses Zorveus, then copy the full prompt into your coding agent. The prompts apply to any project.

- **Business**: Your product pays for customer AI usage. Includes attribution, allowances, plan-specific keys, and billing or webhook work when your product uses those features.
- **Developer OAuth**: Users connect their own Zorveus accounts and authorize your tool to use their funding context. Includes consent, PKCE, token ownership, and revocation.

<IntegrationPromptPicker />

## Before you give the prompt to an agent

Tell the agent which AI features to integrate and any models you have selected from [the model catalog](https://www.zorveus.com/models). Keep secrets in your project's approved secret store. Do not paste keys into the prompt.

The Business prompt adapts the full Zorveus integration-agent prompt. The OAuth version uses a separate workflow so the agent does not apply Business credit management to user-funded connections.
