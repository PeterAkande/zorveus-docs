## Use the gateway

Call AI models with one API key. Point your SDK at `https://api.zorveus.com/v1` and start making requests.

Follow [Make your first API request](/getting-started/startup-quickstart) to get your key and run a call.

## Build for your customers

When your product needs customer limits or user-funded access, choose the path that fits:

| Your integration | Your product pays | Your users pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Example | Your \$5 subscription includes a \$2 monthly AI allowance. | Users connect their Zorveus account to use AI in your app. |
| What your app does | Track each customer's usage, set limits, and connect them to your paid plans. | Let users approve access, then make requests with their token. |
| Continue with | [Manage customer AI usage](/getting-started/integrate-your-product) | [Let users fund their AI usage](/getting-started/oauth-app-quickstart) |
