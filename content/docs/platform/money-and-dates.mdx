# Money and Dates Representation

Zorveus adheres to strict formatting standards across all data representations.

---

## Monetary Values

- **Exact Decimal Strings**: All currency amounts (balances, caps, costs, grant amounts) are encoded as fixed-point decimal strings (e.g. `"10.00"`, `"0.000150"`).
- **Zero Floating Point Inaccuracies**: Decimal strings eliminate binary floating-point representation artifacts across diverse client programming languages.

---

## Timestamps

- **ISO 8601 with UTC Offset**: All dates and timestamps are timezone-aware UTC strings:
  ```text
  2026-08-13T20:45:00Z
  ```
