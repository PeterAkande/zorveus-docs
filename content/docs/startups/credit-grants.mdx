title: Credit Grants
description: Issue consumable, expiring AI credit balances to specific product users.
---

Credit grants allocate dedicated dollar balances from your startup organization's budget to specific product users (`external_user_id`).

Grants allow startups to offer welcome promotions, monthly subscription allocations, or customer support credits without building custom billing meters.

<KeyInserter type="service" label="Service Key Inserter" placeholder="zrv_service_your_service_key" />

---

## Balance Deduction Hierarchy

When a product user sends an inference request, Zorveus deducts usage costs using the following order:

1. **Active Credit Grants (Earliest Expiration First).** Zorveus burns active non-expired credit grants assigned to the user.
2. **Organization Wallet Balance.** Once all active credit grants for the product user are exhausted, requests debit your organization's primary wallet.
3. **Spend Limit Enforcement.** If the user has reached their monthly spend cap or your organization wallet balance is zero, the request fails with an HTTP `402 Payment Required` or `429 Limit Exceeded` response.

## Issue a Credit Grant

<ApiEndpoint method="POST" path="/product-users/{product_user_id}/credit-grants" auth="service_key" />

<CodeGroup titles={["TypeScript (@zorveus/sdk)", "Python (zorveus)", "cURL", "Python (Requests)", "Node.js (Fetch)"]}>

```typescript
import { ZorveusServiceClient } from "@zorveus/sdk";

const service = new ZorveusServiceClient({
  apiKey: "zrv_sk_your_service_key",
});

const grant = await service.productUsers.grantCreditByExternalId({
  appId: "app_startup_123",
  externalUserId: "usr_cust_94821",
  amount: "10.000000000000",
  source: "promotion",
  reason: "Welcome promotion credit grant",
});

console.log("New Balance:", grant.credit_summary.available_credits);
```

```python
from zorveus import ZorveusServiceClient

grant = service.product_users.grant_credit_by_external_id(
    app_id="app_startup_123",
    external_user_id="usr_cust_94821",
    amount="10.000000000000",
    source="promotion",
    reason="Welcome promotion credit grant",
)

print("New Balance:", grant.credit_summary.available_credits)
```

## Webhook Notifications

Whenever a credit grant is added or fully consumed by a product user, Zorveus emits outbound webhooks:

* `product_user.credits_granted`: Triggered when a new credit grant is issued.
* `product_user.cap_reached`: Triggered when available credits and spend limits are hit.

For setup details, see the [Outbound Webhooks Guide](/platform/webhooks).
