title: Configure product-user allowances
description: Set customer AI spending limits, choose enforcement modes, and control overrun policies.
---

When your product pays for your customers' AI usage, Zorveus gives each customer a dedicated spending allowance and stops requests when they exceed their quota.

You configure limits on the connection in the dashboard, and your backend tags each customer request with an external user ID.

## Core concepts

- **Connection cap**: The monthly or daily allowance granted to each customer on this connection. A $10 monthly cap gives every customer their own $10 limit. It is not a shared pool.
- **Credit mode**: Determines whether requests without customer IDs are allowed or blocked.
- **Overrun policy**: Controls what happens if an individual response costs more than a customer's remaining allowance.
- **Direct user caps**: An optional custom cap for a high-volume enterprise customer that overrides the connection default.

---

## Recommended default configuration

For most SaaS applications with tiered subscription plans, start with these settings:

| Setting | Recommended value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Allowed models | Selected models (e.g. `openai/gpt-4.1-mini`) | Prevents unintended access to costly frontier models. |
| Cap amount and period | Your plan allowance (e.g. `$10/month`) | Sets a predictable monthly budget per user. |
| `credit_mode` | `enforce` | Enforces quota limits across all customer requests. |
| `overrun_policy` | `limit_output` | Caps output length to keep total cost within remaining budget. |
| External user ID | Your internal customer ID on every request | Attributes token usage to the correct customer ledger. |

---

## Credit enforcement modes

The credit mode tells Zorveus how strictly to enforce spending limits:

| Mode | Behavior | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `enforce` (Recommended) | Enforces base allowance plus valid credits. Requires attribution. | Production customer traffic where spending limits must be guaranteed. |
| `enforce_if_present` | Enforces allowance when a customer ID is present; allows unattributed calls. | Shared keys serving both internal jobs and customer requests. |
| `track_only` | Records customer usage and credits, but never blocks requests. | Observing customer AI consumption before launching strict quotas. |
| `disabled` | Bypasses all quota checks and credit consumption. | Unrestricted testing environments. |

---

## Overrun policies

AI models generate variable output lengths. If a customer has $0.10 remaining, but their prompt produces a response costing $0.15, the overrun policy determines what happens:

| Policy | Behavior | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `limit_output` (Default) | Automatically reduces maximum generation tokens so the response stays within budget. | Keeping spending strictly within customer limits. |
| `allow_overrun` | Completes the response normally. The extra $0.05 is logged as uncovered spend, but does not penalize future cycles. | Chat apps where delivering a complete answer is more important than small overages. |
| `carry_overrun_forward` | Completes the response, but carries the $0.05 excess as debt. The debt is deducted from next month's allowance or purchased credits. | Strict accounting where every cent of overage must eventually be settled. |

## Common scenarios

### Free vs. Pro subscription tiers

Create two separate connections in your dashboard:

1. **Free connection**: Cap of $2/month, access to lightweight models, `enforce` mode.
2. **Pro connection**: Cap of $20/month, access to frontier models, `enforce` mode.

Your backend inspects the authenticated user's subscription in your database, selects the appropriate API key, and attaches their ID:

```bash
# Pro user request
curl https://api.zorveus.com/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ZORVEUS_PRO_KEY" \
  -H "X-Zorveus-External-User-ID: usr_pro_987" \
  -d '{"model": "anthropic/claude-3-5-sonnet", "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Analyze data"}]}'
```

If a customer upgrades mid-month, your backend immediately starts sending their requests with the Pro key.

### Using one-time credits alongside monthly allowances

When a customer holds both a recurring allowance and purchased credit grants, Zorveus always spends the recurring base allowance first:

```text
Monthly base allowance remaining: $2.00
Purchased credit pack:             $10.00
-----------------------------------------
Total spendable allowance:        $12.00
```

If the customer makes a request that costs $3.00:
1. $2.00 is deducted from their base allowance (exhausting it for the cycle).
2. The remaining $1.00 is deducted from their purchased credit pack.
3. Their new spendable allowance is $9.00.

At the start of the next billing cycle, their $10.00 monthly base allowance resets automatically, while their remaining $9.00 in purchased credits remains available.

### Aligning monthly resets with customer subscription dates

By default, monthly caps reset on the 1st of each calendar month. 

If a customer subscribes on March 18th, set their `allowance_starts_at` anchor to `2026-03-18T00:00:00Z` when creating or updating their profile. Zorveus will automatically reset their $10 allowance on the 18th of every month:

```typescript
await service.productUsers.createOrUpdate({
  appId: "app_123",
  externalUserId: "customer_42",
  allowanceStartsAt: "2026-03-18T00:00:00Z",
});
```
