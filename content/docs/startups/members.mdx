# Team Members and Roles

Organizations can invite multiple team members with distinct role-based access control (RBAC).

---

## Member Roles

| Role | Permissions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Owner** | Full administrative rights, organization deletion, billing ownership, member invites. |
| **Admin** | Manage apps, inference keys, provider credentials, and product users. |
| **Member** | View apps, view usage reports, and generate personal developer keys. |

---

## Product Users vs Organization Members

<Callout type="note" title="Distinct Entities">
  **Organization Members** are your colleagues logging in to [app.zorveus.com](https://app.zorveus.com). **Product Users** are external end users of your software. These concepts are completely separate in Zorveus.
</Callout>
