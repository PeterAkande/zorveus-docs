---
title: Team members and product users
description: Separate dashboard access from customer usage attribution.
---

Team members are colleagues who sign in to your organization in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com). Give them only the permissions needed for their work. Manage membership and invitations in the dashboard

Product users are your application's customers. They do not need dashboard accounts. Your backend identifies them with `X-Zorveus-External-User-ID` or supported inference metadata

Use [product users](/startups/product-users) for customer allowance and usage. Use organization membership for people who configure apps, credentials, or funding
