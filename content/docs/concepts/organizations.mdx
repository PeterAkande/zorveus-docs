# Organizations

In Zorveus, an **Organization** is the fundamental security, ownership, and funding boundary. All apps, wallets, provider credentials, product users, and members belong to an organization.

---

## Organization Types

| Type | Intended Audience | Spendable Wallet? | Primary Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`startup`** | AI Product Teams & Companies | Yes | Create internal apps, issue inference keys, manage product users, store BYOK credentials, grant credits. |
| **`developer`** | Third-Party Tool Creators | No (aggregate only) | Register OAuth applications, configure redirect URIs, manage test users, review OAuth consent logs. |
| **`personal`** | Individual AI Consumers | Yes | Fund personal wallet, manage connected OAuth applications, set personal spending caps. |

<Callout type="note" title="Internal Organizations">
  The `internal` organization type is reserved strictly for Zorveus platform infrastructure operations and is never available as a selectable customer workspace.
</Callout>

---

## Ownership and Isolation

- **Isolated Wallets**: Each `startup` and `personal` organization owns a distinct ledger wallet with independent balances and currency rules. Funds cannot automatically spill over between organizations.
- **Member Access**: Users can belong to multiple organizations (e.g. your personal wallet and your company's startup workspace) with distinct role permissions in each.
- **Provider Credentials**: BYOK credentials added to Organization A are completely invisible and unrouteable by Organization B, even if both organizations are managed by the same team members.
