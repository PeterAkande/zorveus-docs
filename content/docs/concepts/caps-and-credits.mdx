# Caps and Credits

Zorveus provides two distinct controls to govern AI expenditures: **Spending Caps** and **Credit Grants**.

---

## Caps vs. Credits at a Glance

| Feature | Spending Cap | Credit Grant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Hard ceiling on financial liability | Allocate consumable promotional or paid value |
| **Reset Behavior** | Resets periodically (e.g. monthly) | Non-replenishing (consumes balance until 0) |
| **Applied To** | Organization, App Connection, Product User | Product User |
| **Enforcement** | Blocks requests once ceiling is reached | Blocks requests when credit balance is exhausted |
| **Funding Source** | Organization Wallet or BYOK | Startup Organization budget |

---

## 1. Spending Caps

A **Spending Cap** specifies a maximum allowable spend over a rolling window (e.g. `$25.00` per month):
- Evaluated prior to pre-flight token reservation.
- If the current period's spend plus the pending reservation exceeds the cap, the gateway rejects the request with `429 Too Many Requests` (`error.code: "cap_exceeded"`).
- Caps can be configured at three distinct levels:
  1. **Organization Cap**: Overall organization spend limit.
  2. **Connection / Key Cap**: Specific limit for a single app connection or OAuth token.
  3. **Product-User Cap**: Per-user limit enforcing fair use across your customer base.

---

## 2. Product-User Credit Grants

A **Credit Grant** assigns a finite dollar value (e.g. `$5.00` trial credit) to a product user:
- Multiple grants stack additively.
- Grants with the earliest expiration date are debited first (FIFO).
- You can revoke remaining balances at any time via the Management API.
- Zorveus remains agnostic to your application tier names (Free, Pro, Enterprise); you translate your billing rules into grants.
