---
title: Funding, caps, and credits
description: Separate who pays for AI requests (wallet or BYOK) from how much each customer is allowed to spend (caps, credits, and cost tracking).
---

The most important rule in Zorveus billing is separating **funding** from **allowance**:

- **Funding** answers: *Who pays the model provider?* (Your Zorveus organization wallet, your own BYOK provider account, or a connected user's wallet.)
- **Allowance** answers: *How much AI usage may this customer consume?* (Their spending cap plus any extra customer credits.)

Because these two layers are independent, a customer can still have allowance left while your organization wallet is empty (`402 Payment Required`), or your wallet can be funded while a customer hits their personal limit (`403 Forbidden`).

---

## Two ways to fund requests

### 1. Zorveus wallet

Zorveus routes the request through managed provider access and bills your organization wallet:

1. Before calling the model, Zorveus temporarily holds an estimated charge from your available balance.
2. After the model finishes, Zorveus charges actual token usage and releases any unused hold.
3. If your wallet cannot cover the estimate, the gateway rejects the call with HTTP `402 Payment Required` (`zorveus_reservation_insufficient_balance`).

### 2. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

Zorveus injects your organization's own provider key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and others) at request time:

- Your upstream provider bills your provider account directly.
- Customer caps and credits still run on the exact same reference pricing as wallet-funded calls.
- Once monthly BYOK volume exceeds your organization's free threshold, Zorveus deducts a platform routing fee from your organization wallet. Customer credit grants never pay platform fees.

See [Provider keys (BYOK)](/startups/provider-credentials) and [BYOK costs](/wallet-and-billing/byok-costs) for setup and fee details.

## Caps vs. customer credits

| | Spending cap | Credit grant |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purpose** | Repeating or lifetime base allowance on an organization, API key, or customer. | Extra consumable allowance granted to one customer. |
| **Reset behavior** | Daily and monthly caps reset automatically. Lifetime caps do not reset. | Does not replenish; spent down to zero or expires on its end date. |
| **Consumption order** | Consumed first. | Consumed after the base cap runs out (soonest-expiring grant first). |
| **Funding effect** | Ceiling only, not cash. | Extends customer allowance only; does not add cash to your wallet. |

If an API key has no spending cap configured, customer allowance is unlimited and customer credits are not consumed. See [Spending caps](/wallet-and-billing/caps) for worked examples and overrun policies (`limit_output`, `allow_overrun`, `carry_overrun_forward`).

## Credit enforcement modes (`credit_mode`)

Each API key configures how Zorveus enforces caps and customer credits:

| Mode | With customer ID | Without customer ID | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `enforce_if_present` (default) | Enforces cap + credits | Skips cap checks, still logs usage | Recommended for keys serving both customer and internal requests. |
| `enforce` | Enforces cap + credits | Enforces key-level cap | Enforces limits on every request. |
| `track_only` | Tracks usage without blocking | Tracks usage without blocking | Never rejects for allowance; logs spend past the cap as uncovered. |
| `disabled` | Skips cap and credit checks | Skips cap and credit checks | Logs usage for analytics with no quota enforcement. |

User-funded OAuth tokens always run in `enforce` mode.

## The three cost figures and prompt caching

Every completed request records an immutable usage event with three separate dollar figures (always stored as exact decimal strings):

| Field | What it tracks | Wallet mode | BYOK mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `virtual_spend` | Reference-priced usage counted against caps and customer credits. | Yes | Yes |
| `sell_cost` | Actual cash deducted from your Zorveus organization wallet. | Yes | Platform fee only (`$0.00` below threshold) |
| `provider_cost` | Wholesale upstream provider cost for reporting and fee math. | Yes | Yes |

When a provider reports prompt caching, Zorveus also records `cache_read_input_tokens`, `cache_creation_input_tokens`, `cache_creation_1h_input_tokens`, and `cache_savings`. On BYOK requests, `cache_savings` measures reference-priced allowance saved rather than a discount on your upstream invoice.

See [Usage analytics](/wallet-and-billing/usage) to inspect these fields in the dashboard.
