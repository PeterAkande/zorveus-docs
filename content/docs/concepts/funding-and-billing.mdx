# Funding and Billing Models

Zorveus provides three distinct billing modes to support diverse commercial architectures.

---

## 1. Zorveus-Funded Wallet Usage

In the standard wallet-funded mode, Zorveus acts as the merchant of record for AI inference:
- **Available vs Reserved Balance**: Before sending a request to the upstream provider, Zorveus computes a pre-flight reservation from your organization wallet.
- **Settlement**: When the provider finishes streaming tokens, the actual token count and sell price are settled, and unused reserved funds are released immediately.
- **Provider Cost vs Sell Cost**: Both metrics are recorded. The difference represents the Zorveus routing margin.

```text
[Pre-Flight Request]  ---> Reserve $0.0050 from Wallet
[Streaming Tokens]    ---> Provider returns 450 tokens
[Post-Flight Settle]  ---> Settle $0.0032 actual debit, release $0.0018 unspent
```

---

## 2. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

When your organization attaches its own provider credentials (e.g. your company's direct OpenAI or Anthropic account):
- **Zorveus Sell Cost**: `$0.000000` (Zorveus does not debit your wallet for model tokens).
- **Direct Upstream Invoice**: The model provider invoices your corporate credit card or enterprise contract directly.
- **Token Accounting**: Zorveus continues to track exact prompt/completion tokens, provider cost estimates, and product-user attribution.

---

## 3. Product-User Credits

When an app connection enforces product-user credits:
- The inference request must include an identifiable product user (`external_user_id` or `product_user_id`).
- The product user must hold an active, non-expired credit grant with sufficient remaining balance.
- Consumed tokens debit the product user's credit balance first.
- The startup funds the underlying organization wallet or BYOK credentials; credit grants represent internal allocation to customers.

---

## Money Representation

<Callout type="note" title="Decimal Precision">
  All monetary values in the Zorveus API and usage events are formatted as exact decimal strings (e.g. `"0.004210"`) to avoid IEEE floating-point rounding errors across client languages.
</Callout>
