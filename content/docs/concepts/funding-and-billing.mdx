Zorveus provides three primary billing mechanisms: organization wallet funding, Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) routing, and end-user credit grants.

## Wallet-funded inference

Zorveus supports inference requests funded directly from your organization wallet. The wallet maintains a prepaid balance that settles inference requests across model providers.

When an inference request arrives, the gateway verifies active balance and spending caps. If funds are sufficient, the request executes and the exact token cost is deducted from the wallet ledger upon stream completion.

---

## Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

With BYOK, Zorveus routes requests using your organization's encrypted API credentials (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini).

- **Upstream billing**: The upstream provider bills your credit card or billing account directly for model tokens.
- **$25,000/month free allowance**: Every organization receives up to **$25,000/month** in free BYOK routing (`sell_cost = $0.00`).
- **Volume tiers (> $25,000/month)**: A configured volume platform fee applies only to the incremental volume exceeding $25k/month, settled against your Zorveus wallet balance.

---

## Product-user credits

Credit grants allocate a promotional or plan-based spending allowance to a specific end user (`external_user_id`).

Whether exhausting a credit balance blocks subsequent requests depends on the connection's `credit_mode`:
- `strict_enforcement`: Over-budget requests are rejected immediately when the user's credits and allowance expire.
- `unrestricted`: The organization wallet continues to fund overage without blocking the user.

All financial amounts returned by the API are decimal strings. Use a decimal-compatible library for client-side calculations and refer to the wallet ledger for authoritative settlement.
