---
title: BYOK cost accounting
description: Understand the wallet and provider costs recorded for requests that use your own provider credentials.
---

When a request uses a stored provider credential, Zorveus records the request with the `byok` billing mode. The usage event separates two costs:

- `provider_cost` is the estimated upstream model cost recorded for analytics.
- `sell_cost` is the amount charged to the Zorveus wallet. It can include a configured BYOK platform fee.

Do not assume that every BYOK request has a zero `sell_cost`. Check the usage event and your organization’s current billing configuration.

Your upstream provider bills the credential owner separately. Zorveus wallet balances do not represent or settle that provider invoice.
Zorveus offer Free routing up to $25k in monthly traffic. If your organization exceeds that threshold, Zorveus can apply a configured BYOK platform fee. Check the usage event and your organization’s current billing configuration.
