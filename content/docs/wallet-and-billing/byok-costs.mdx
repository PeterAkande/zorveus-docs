When your organization routes requests using Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) credentials, upstream model providers bill your accounts directly while Zorveus provides gateway routing, spend caps, and attribution.

## Monthly pricing tiers

Zorveus includes a free monthly routing allowance for all BYOK traffic:

| Monthly BYOK traffic volume | Zorveus platform fee | Upstream provider billing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First $25,000 / month** | **$0.00** (`sell_cost = $0.00`) | Upstream provider bills you directly |
| **Above $25,000 / month** | Configured volume platform fee | Upstream provider bills you directly |

<Callout type="info" title="Free Tier Threshold">
  Every organization receives **$25,000 per month** of free BYOK routing traffic. The Zorveus platform fee only applies to the incremental volume exceeding $25,000 within a calendar month.
</Callout>

## How usage accounting works

For every inference request routed through a BYOK credential, the Zorveus gateway records a usage event containing two distinct cost fields:

### 1. Provider cost (`provider_cost`)
* Represents the estimated cost charged by the upstream model provider (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini).
* Recorded purely for analytics, customer attribution, and spend cap enforcement.
* **Not debited from your Zorveus wallet** (the upstream provider bills your credit card or invoice directly).

### 2. Sell cost (`sell_cost`)
* Represents the amount debited from your Zorveus organization wallet.
* Under the $25k/month free tier, `sell_cost` is `$0.00`.
* For volume exceeding $25k/month, `sell_cost` reflects the configured platform routing fee.

## What happens when you exceed $25k/month

1. **Continuous routing**: Your AI traffic is never interrupted when crossing the $25,000 monthly threshold.
2. **Platform fee settlement**: The configured platform fee for volume above $25k is automatically deducted from your organization's Zorveus prepaid wallet balance.
3. **Wallet balance reminders**: Maintain a funded Zorveus wallet balance if your organization anticipates monthly BYOK traffic above $25,000.
