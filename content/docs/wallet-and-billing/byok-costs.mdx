---
title: BYOK costs and accounting
description: Separate upstream provider billing, virtual allowance, and Zorveus platform fees.
---

With BYOK, the upstream model provider bills your provider account. Zorveus records usage, applies your spending policy, and can charge a platform routing fee

## Read the three amounts

| Amount | Meaning for BYOK |
| --- | --- |
| `virtual_spend` | Reference-priced usage applied to caps and customer allowance |
| `provider_cost` | Recorded upstream usage cost, used for reporting and platform-fee calculation |
| `sell_cost` | Amount charged to your Zorveus wallet, including any platform fee |

Virtual spend uses the same reference pricing for wallet-funded and BYOK usage. Switching to BYOK does not make customer allowance unlimited. Provider cost reported by Zorveus is not a replacement for your provider's invoice

Successful BYOK usage is recorded as `billing_mode: "byok_external"`. A managed fallback request records `wallet` and charges the wallet-funded model price

## Understand the monthly threshold

The monthly free routing threshold and overage fee percentage are configurable. Check the pricing that applies to your organization before forecasting costs. A local backend default is not a guarantee of production pricing

Only provider cost above the configured monthly threshold contributes to the platform fee:

```text
feeable cost = max(0, monthly cost after request - threshold)
             - max(0, monthly cost before request - threshold)
platform fee = feeable cost * fee percentage / 100
```

For example, assume a $100 threshold, a 5% fee, and $99 of provider cost already recorded this month. A request adding $2 crosses the threshold by $1, so its platform fee is $0.05. These are illustrative values, not a pricing offer

## Keep funding separate from allowance

The organization wallet pays applicable platform fees. Product-user credits extend virtual allowance and cannot pay those fees or the upstream bill

The gateway can reserve an estimated BYOK fee before dispatch. Insufficient wallet funding returns `402` with `zorveus_reservation_insufficient_balance`. Maintain wallet funds for feeable traffic and any configured managed fallback

For credential ordering and fallback conditions, see [Automatic provider routing](/startups/automatic-routing). For a customer purchase that grants virtual credits, see [Billing integration](/startups/billing-integration)
