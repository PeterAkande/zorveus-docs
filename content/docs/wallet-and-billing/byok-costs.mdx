---
title: BYOK costs
description: Understand how upstream provider bills, customer allowances, and Zorveus platform fees work with Bring Your Own Key.
---

When you route requests through your own provider keys (BYOK), the upstream provider bills your provider account directly. Zorveus tracks usage, enforces your spending caps and customer allowances, and charges a platform routing fee once your monthly BYOK volume crosses your organization's free threshold.

---

## Read the three amounts

| Field | What it means for BYOK |
| :--- | :--- |
| `virtual_spend` | Reference-priced usage counted against spending caps and customer allowances. |
| `provider_cost` | Estimated upstream provider cost used for reporting and platform-fee math. |
| `sell_cost` | Amount charged to your Zorveus wallet, including any applicable platform fee. |

`virtual_spend` uses the same reference pricing whether a request runs on BYOK or your Zorveus wallet, so switching to BYOK does not make customer allowances unlimited. `provider_cost` in Zorveus is for tracking and fee calculation and does not replace your upstream provider's invoice.

- Successful BYOK requests record `billing_mode: "byok_external"`.
- Requests that fall back to Zorveus's managed route record `billing_mode: "wallet"` and charge the wallet-funded model price.

---

## Monthly free threshold and platform fee

The monthly free routing threshold and overage fee percentage are configurable per organization. Check your organization's active pricing in the dashboard before forecasting costs.

Only provider cost above the monthly threshold incurs the platform fee:

```text
feeable cost = max(0, monthly cost after request - threshold)
             - max(0, monthly cost before request - threshold)
platform fee = feeable cost * fee percentage / 100
```

For example, with an illustrative `$100` monthly threshold, a `5%` fee, and `$99` of provider cost already recorded this month, a `$2` request crosses the threshold by `$1` and incurs a `$0.05` platform fee.

---

## Keep wallet funds available for fees and fallback

Your organization wallet pays any applicable BYOK platform fees and any requests served through wallet fallback. Product-user credit grants only extend customer allowances and cannot pay platform fees or upstream invoices.

Before dispatching a feeable BYOK request, Zorveus can reserve the estimated platform fee from your wallet. If your wallet balance is too low, the request returns HTTP `402 Payment Required` (`zorveus_reservation_insufficient_balance`). Keep funds in your wallet whenever you expect feeable BYOK volume or enable `auto_fallback_to_wallet`.
