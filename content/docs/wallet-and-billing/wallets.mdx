Every spendable organization has a wallet that pays for managed model requests and any applicable BYOK platform fees. Manage your balance, top-ups, promotional codes, and ledger history in the [Zorveus dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com).

Your organization wallet is separate from customer AI allowances. Customer credit grants increase a customer's spending allowance inside your product; they do not add cash to your organization wallet.

---

## Available vs. reserved balance

Before Zorveus sends a request to a model provider, it holds an estimated charge from your wallet:

- **`available_balance`**: Funds ready for new requests.
- **`reserved_balance`**: Funds temporarily held for requests currently in flight.

For example, a $50.00 wallet with $1.80 held for active calls leaves $48.20 available. When a request completes, Zorveus charges actual usage and releases any unused hold back to `available_balance`.

A slow or disconnected stream can keep a hold open until the provider settles or Zorveus reconciles it. Do not assume a client disconnect stopped the upstream model from producing billable output.

---

## Fund your wallet

1. Open the [Zorveus dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) and select the organization that pays for usage.
2. Open **Wallet** and start a top-up using any payment method and currency offered at checkout.
3. Confirm that the credited amount appears in your available balance before relying on it for traffic.

### Confirming a top-up

Treat the checkout return page as a status view, not proof of payment. Zorveus verifies provider payment confirmation before crediting your wallet, and pending payments can settle asynchronously.

Wait for either the updated balance in the dashboard or the signed `wallet.payment_credited` webhook before treating funds as available. That webhook tracks a Zorveus organization wallet purchase, not a customer payment to your own product. For customer purchases, see [Grant credits after a payment](/startups/credit-grants#grant-credits-after-a-payment) and [Webhooks](/platform/webhooks).

## Promotional credits

When a Zorveus promotion is active, redeem the code in **Wallet** in the dashboard. Eligibility, redemption limits, valid dates, and expiry depend on the campaign, and redeeming a code does not add withdrawable cash.

To issue promotional AI allowance to your own customers, create a [product-user credit grant](/startups/credit-grants) with the appropriate source and expiration instead.

---

## Wallet ledger

Open the **Ledger** tab under **Wallet** in the dashboard to inspect every wallet movement: top-ups, in-flight reservations, settled usage charges, released holds, and adjustments.

Customer credit grants and allowance changes have their own separate history. Use the [product-user API](/api-reference/product-users) or **Product Users** in the dashboard to inspect customer balances and grants.

---

## Fix funding failures

If your wallet cannot cover an estimated reservation, model requests fail with HTTP `402 Payment Required` (`zorveus_reservation_insufficient_balance`). Top up the paying organization's wallet and confirm the credited available balance before retrying.

An organization can also hit HTTP `402` (`zorveus_org_billing_frozen`) if billing is frozen or wallet debt is unresolved. Clear that status in the dashboard before sending new traffic. Granting customer credits does not resolve organization wallet debt.
