A spendable organization has a wallet that funds managed inference and applicable BYOK platform fees. Fund and inspect it in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com)

## Available and reserved funds

`available_balance` is available for new reservations. `reserved_balance` is held for in-flight requests. Their sum is the balance before those pending charges settle

For example, a $50 balance with $1.80 held leaves $48.20 available. The hold is an estimate. Settlement charges actual usage and releases any unused amount

A slow or disconnected request can need later reconciliation before all holds are released. Do not treat a stream disconnect as proof that the provider stopped producing billable output

## Restore funding

If the wallet cannot cover an estimated charge, inference can return `402` with `zorveus_reservation_insufficient_balance`. Top up the funding organization's wallet and confirm the credited available balance before retrying

An organization can also have a billing freeze or outstanding wallet debt. Resolve that state before new inference. A customer credit grant does not clear wallet debt or add cash

See [Wallet top-ups](/wallet-and-billing/top-ups), [BYOK costs](/wallet-and-billing/byok-costs), and [Funding and billing](/concepts/funding-and-billing)
