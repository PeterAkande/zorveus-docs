---
title: Fund your wallet
description: Add funds in the dashboard and verify that they are available.
---

Select the organization that will pay for inference in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com). Open its wallet and start a top-up. Use the payment methods and currencies offered by that checkout

A checkout return or payment attempt is not proof that the wallet was credited. Confirm the available balance before relying on it for new inference. A pending payment can require later provider confirmation

To grant allowance after a customer pays your own product, follow [Billing integration](/startups/billing-integration). Your customer's payment and an organization wallet top-up are separate operations
