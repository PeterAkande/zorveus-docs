# Spending Caps Management

<ApiEndpoint method="PUT" path="/caps" auth="dashboard_session" />

Spending caps act as circuit breakers to prevent unforeseen bills.

---

## Configuring Caps

- **Organization Cap**: Overall budget cap for the company.
- **Connection Cap**: Spending limit per application or OAuth connection.
- **Product-User Cap**: Max monthly spend allowed per end user.
