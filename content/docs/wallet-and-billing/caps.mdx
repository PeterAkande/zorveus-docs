---
title: Spending caps
description: Configure monthly spending limits for organizations, app connections, and product users.
---

Spending caps stop new reservations after the applicable monthly limit is reached. A cap is a guardrail, not a prepaid balance.

## Cap scopes

- An organization cap limits aggregate organization spend.
- An app-connection cap limits aggregate spend through that connection and all keys attached to it.
- A product-user cap limits the attributed end user’s spend.

Product-user credit grants are separate from caps. A grant creates spendable credit for one product user; a cap limits how much that user may spend during the period.

When more than one cap applies, the request must fit within every limit. Existing settled usage is not reversed when you lower a cap.
