# Wallet Ledger

<ApiEndpoint method="GET" path="/wallet/ledger" auth="dashboard_session" />

The Zorveus Ledger records every balance modification as an immutable transaction.

---

## Ledger Entry Types

- `top_up`: Inbound payment funds credited.
- `inference_reservation`: Temporary hold during streaming.
- `inference_settlement`: Final settled token charge.
- `promotion_credit`: Promotional grant applied.
- `refund`: Balance adjustment or reversal.
