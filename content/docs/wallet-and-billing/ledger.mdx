---
title: Wallet ledger
description: Distinguish wallet movements from customer allowance and credit history.
---

Inspect wallet activity in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com). The ledger records funding, holds for in-flight requests, usage charges, released funds, and applicable adjustments

A reservation temporarily moves funds out of the available balance. Settlement charges actual usage and releases unused funds. A slow or interrupted request can leave a hold pending until it is reconciled

Customer credit grants have separate allowance history. They do not change wallet cash. Use the [product-user API](/api-reference/product-users) to inspect current allowance, credits, and grants. Use the dashboard for wallet transaction details
