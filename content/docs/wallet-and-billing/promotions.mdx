# Promotional Codes

Organizations can redeem promotional credit vouchers to receive startup credits.

---

## Eligibility Criteria

Promotional campaigns enforce strict targeting criteria:
- Verified corporate email address
- Organization type matching (e.g. `startup` organizations only)
- Single redemption per organization boundary
- Time-bounded redemption window and credit expiration
