---
title: Promotional credits
description: Distinguish wallet promotions from customer allowance grants.
---

Redeem a Zorveus promotional code in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) when a campaign is available. Eligibility, redemption limits, dates, and credit expiry depend on that campaign. A code does not guarantee eligibility or an immediate cash wallet balance

To issue promotional AI allowance to your own customers, create a [product-user credit grant](/startups/credit-grants) with the appropriate source and expiration. That grant does not fund your organization's wallet
