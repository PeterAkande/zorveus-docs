## Which organization do I need?

Choose Business when your backend pays for AI, including a solo project. Choose Developer to register an OAuth app whose users authorize their own funding. See [Getting started](/getting-started/overview)

## Which key do I send to inference?

Use an inference key, routing key, or scoped OAuth access token. A service key is for backend product-user operations and cannot call inference. See [Authentication](/platform/authentication)

## Does a connection cap give each customer their own allowance?

For attributed Business requests, Zorveus evaluates the connection's cap separately for each product user. For example, a $10 monthly connection cap supplies a $10 base allowance to each attributed customer. Usage in that customer's current app allowance window can span plan connections, so switching keys does not erase usage

Without attribution, an enforced connection cap covers that connection's unattributed traffic. With `enforce_if_present`, missing attribution bypasses virtual allowance checks. Validate the customer ID in your backend. A direct product-user cap can override the allowance shown on their profile, and other applicable caps can still constrain admission

See [Configure product-user allowances](/startups/configure-product-user-allowances)

## Must I register product users first?

No. Send your authenticated customer ID with inference, for example in `X-Zorveus-External-User-ID`. A service-key profile read returns `404` until that app and ID exist. You can also create the profile with a service-key upsert

## Do credit grants fund my wallet?

No. Grants extend a customer's virtual AI allowance. Your wallet or upstream provider account still pays for model usage. See [Funding and billing](/concepts/funding-and-billing)

## Can I mix BYOK and wallet-funded requests?

Yes. Eligible credentials can route requests through your upstream account. Wallet fallback after a BYOK failure depends on its configuration and funding. See [Automatic provider routing](/startups/automatic-routing)

## Is BYOK free?

The upstream provider bills your provider account. Zorveus can charge a configured platform fee above the monthly free routing threshold. See [BYOK costs](/wallet-and-billing/byok-costs)

## Does Zorveus add latency?

Requests pass through the gateway for authentication, routing, and accounting. End-to-end latency depends on the model, provider, network, and request. Measure with your workload rather than assuming a fixed gateway overhead

## What is in a usage record?

Usage records contain accounting and attribution fields such as model, token counts, costs, status, and identifiers. This does not describe the retention policy of every provider or optional logging integration. Review the data-handling settings for the route you use before sending sensitive data
