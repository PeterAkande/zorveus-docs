title: Frequently asked questions
description: Answers about credentials, product users, billing, Bring Your Own Key routing, latency, and stored usage data.
---

### Does Zorveus add latency to AI inference?

Zorveus adds negligible gateway processing and network transit to the provider request. Streaming endpoints return Server-Sent Events in real time. Latency depends primarily on the upstream provider, selected model, and geographical region.

---

### Can I mix BYOK keys and wallet-funded routing?

Yes. An eligible provider credential routes matching models through your upstream provider account. If `auto_fallback_to_wallet` is enabled and your organization wallet is funded, Zorveus automatically falls back to wallet-funded routing if your BYOK key encounters rate limits or quota exhaustion.

---

### Does Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) mean inference is free?

No. Upstream model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini) bill your credit card or invoice directly for model tokens.

Zorveus provides **free BYOK routing for the first $25,000/month** in traffic (`sell_cost = $0.00`). If your monthly BYOK volume exceeds $25k, Zorveus applies a volume platform fee to the incremental traffic above $25k, settled against your Zorveus wallet.

---

### Are prompt inputs and completions stored?

Zorveus usage records contain accounting and observability fields (model, provider, token counts, cost, status, latency, and user attribution). Zorveus does not persist prompt inputs or completion text.

---

### Which key calls inference?

Use an inference key (`zrv_...`) against `https://api.zorveus.com/v1`. Scoped OAuth user tokens are also valid inference credentials.

Do not use an organization Service Key for inference. Service keys (`zrv_service_...` or `zrv_svc_...`) are strictly for programmatic workspace management routes at `https://api.zorveus.com`.

---

### Does an inference-key cap give every customer a separate allowance?

No. An inference key belongs to an app connection, and an app-connection cap limits aggregate usage across that key.

To enforce individual limits for each customer:
- Use **Product-User Spend Caps** for monthly/daily spending caps per customer.
- Use **Product-User Credit Grants** to issue one-time or promotional allowances to specific users.

---

### Must I register product users in advance?

No. Simply pass `metadata.external_user_id` (e.g. `usr_123`) in your inference request payload or SDK client. Zorveus automatically provisions and tracks the product user on the fly.
