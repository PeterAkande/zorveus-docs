# Support & Community

We are here to help you integrate and scale your AI products.

---

## Contact Channels

- **Developer Support**: Email `support@zorveus.com` for technical assistance and onboarding help.
- **Security Vulnerabilities**: Report security issues directly to `security@zorveus.com` (PGP key available on request).
- **System Status**: Check live API uptime and incident history at [status.zorveus.com](https://status.zorveus.com).
- **Community Discord**: Join fellow developers and founders on our [Community Discord](https://discord.gg/zorveus).
