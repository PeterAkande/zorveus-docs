With user-funded apps, each user pays for their own AI requests instead of your company covering model bills. A user connects their Zorveus account to your app, approves the models and spending limit they want to allow, and Zorveus bills their account whenever your app calls a model for them.

You do not pay for their model usage, and you do not manage provider accounts on their behalf.

---

## How it works

User-funded apps use OAuth 2.0 with S256 PKCE to connect the user once, then reuse the returned token on every AI request:

<FlowDiagram flow="oauth-wallet" />

1. **Connect once**: The user clicks your **Connect Zorveus** button, signs in on `app.zorveus.com`, picks the organization that pays, and approves your requested models and spending cap.
2. **Exchange the code**: Zorveus redirects back to your callback URL with a one-time authorization code. Your app sends that code and its PKCE `code_verifier` to `POST https://api.zorveus.com/oauth/token` and gets back a user access token.
3. **Call models**: Your app sends model requests to `https://api.zorveus.com/v1` with the user's access token as the API key. Zorveus checks model permissions and spend limits, then bills the user's wallet.

## Choose your client type

Zorveus OAuth supports two client types, depending on whether your app runs in the browser or on a server:

| | Public client | Confidential client |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Best for** | React apps, single-page apps, mobile apps, desktop tools | Node.js, Next.js server routes, Python, and other backends |
| **Credentials** | `client_id` only. Never has a client secret. | `client_id` and `client_secret`, stored on your server. |
| **PKCE verifier** | Kept in browser memory or `sessionStorage` until callback. | Kept in the signed-in user's server session. |
| **Token exchange** | Calls `POST /oauth/token` without `client_secret`. | Calls `POST /oauth/token` with `client_secret` from your server. |
| **Token storage** | In-memory state (for example via `@zorveus/react`) or browser storage for that user. | Server database or server session tied to the signed-in user. |

### When to use user-funded apps

- **Creation tools**: Writing studios, image editors, audio tools, and research apps where heavy users run unpredictable token volumes.
- **Developer tools and CLIs**: Local coding agents, IDE extensions, and desktop utilities where users supply their own compute.
- **Team and enterprise connectors**: Apps where customer organizations want AI costs billed directly to their own Zorveus account.

If your product collects a subscription or payment through your payment provider and pays for customer AI usage from your own Zorveus wallet, use [Customer limits and billing](/startups/configure-product-user-allowances) instead.
