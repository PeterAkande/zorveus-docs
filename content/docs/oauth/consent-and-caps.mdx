# Consent & Spending Caps

During the OAuth consent screen, the user:
1. **Selects Funding Organization**: Chooses between their personal wallet or any startup organization they own.
2. **Reviews Requested Scopes**: Inspects models and permissions requested by the application.
3. **Sets a Spending Cap**: Configures a hard monthly ceiling (e.g. `$10.00 / month`) or selects Unlimited.

<Callout type="note" title="Developer Cap Boundaries">
  If you configure `min_cap`, `default_cap`, or `max_cap` in your app settings, the consent screen pre-populates these recommendations for the user.
</Callout>
