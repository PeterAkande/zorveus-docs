---
title: Register an OAuth app
description: Create a developer app and configure its redirect URIs, scopes, models, and cap policy.
---

Create an OAuth app in the [Zorveus Dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) to connect your product to Zorveus users. You can configure the app's redirect URIs, scopes, models, and cap policy.

Confidential apps receive a client secret at creation. Store it immediately because secrets are shown only when created or rotated. Public clients use PKCE without storing a client secret.
If you misplace your secret, you can always rotate it on teh app details page.

Redirect URIs use exact matching. Register every environment explicitly and use HTTPS outside approved local development cases.
