# App Review and Publishing

To make your application available to any Zorveus user without test user allowlists, submit your app for review.

---

## App Status Lifecycle

```text
[draft] ---> [submitted] ---> [active] (Verified & Public)
                  |
                  +----------> [rejected] / [suspended]
```

- **`draft`**: Usable only by registered test users.
- **`submitted`**: Under review by the Zorveus team.
- **`active`**: Publicly available and verified.
- **`rejected`**: Review feedback provided in dashboard.
- **`suspended`**: Temporarily deactivated due to policy violations.
