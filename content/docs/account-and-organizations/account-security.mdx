---
title: Account security
description: Protect dashboard access and application credentials.
---

Use the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) to manage your account and credentials. Keep inference keys, routing keys, service keys, and confidential OAuth secrets in your server's secret store

Rotate exposed credentials and replace them in your application. Never include live keys or tokens in support requests. See [Security](/platform/security) for OAuth and webhook guidance
