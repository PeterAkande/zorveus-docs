---
title: Organizations and team access
description: Choose the right organization type, invite team members with scoped roles, and keep dashboard access separate from product users.
---

Every app, wallet, API key, BYOK credential, and customer record in Zorveus belongs to an organization. You can belong to multiple organizations with different roles in each.

---

## Organization types

| Type | API value | What it is for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal** | `personal` | Individual wallet for funding your own connected OAuth apps. |
| **Business** | `startup` | Company workspace that funds AI requests for your product, manages API keys and BYOK credentials, and tracks customer allowances. |
| **Developer** | `developer` | Workspace for registering and publishing OAuth apps where end users fund their own usage. |

Removing or suspending an organization is not self-service. Contact [support](/resources/support) if you need an organization lifecycle change.

---

## Team members vs. product users

- **Team members** are colleagues who sign in to your organization in the [Zorveus dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) to configure apps, keys, billing, or support.
- **Product users** are your application's end customers. They do not need Zorveus dashboard accounts. Your backend identifies them with `X-Zorveus-External-User-ID` (or request metadata) on model calls and manages their balances with [product users](/startups/product-users).

Do not invite your application's customers as organization members.

---

## Member roles

Owners and admins invite team members under **Members** in the [Zorveus dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com) and assign the lowest role needed for their job:

| Role | What they can do | What they cannot do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Owner**<br />`owner` | Full access: manage members, apps, keys, BYOK credentials, webhooks, wallet top-ups, and organization caps. | None. |
| **Admin**<br />`admin` | Manage apps, members, BYOK credentials, webhooks, and product users. View wallet history and set app caps. | Top up the wallet or change organization-level spending caps. |
| **Developer**<br />`developer` | Create and configure apps, API keys, and their own service keys. View redacted diagnostics. | Access customer records or manage company funds. |
| **Billing admin**<br />`billing_admin` | Top up the wallet, read wallet history, set organization and app spending caps, and disconnect connected apps. | Access customer records or BYOK provider credentials. |
| **Member**<br />`member` | View apps, member directory, usage totals, and webhook status. Authorize and disconnect their own connected apps using the org wallet. | Change organization settings or manage customer records. |
| **Spender**<br />`spender` | Use approved AI access through their own connected apps and view only their own usage. | View organization settings, member directory, or company funds. |
| **Viewer**<br />`viewer` | Read-only access to the org profile, member directory, apps, and usage totals. | Change settings or read wallet history, customer records, or BYOK credentials. |

You can change a member's role or remove them from **Members** at any time. Pending invitations can be revoked before they are accepted.
