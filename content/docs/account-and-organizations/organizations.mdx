## Organization types

| Type | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `personal` | A personal wallet that can fund connected OAuth apps. |
| `startup` | A company workspace that funds its own product's inference. |
| `developer` | A workspace that owns OAuth-connectable apps. |

Removing or suspending an organization is not exposed as a public self-service operation. Contact [support](/resources/support) if you need help with an organization lifecycle change.
