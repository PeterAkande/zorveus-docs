---
title: Members and invitations
description: Manage the people who configure your organization.
---

Manage team members and invitations in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com). Give each person only the permissions needed to configure apps, funding, or credentials

Team members sign in to Zorveus. [Product users](/startups/product-users) are your application's customers and are identified by the IDs your backend sends with inference. Do not create a dashboard member for every customer
