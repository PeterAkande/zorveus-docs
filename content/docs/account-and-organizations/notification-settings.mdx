---
title: Notification settings
description: Configure personal email and in-app dashboard alerts for wallet balance, spending caps, payments, and credentials.
---

Notification settings control alerts for **your own Zorveus user account** in the [dashboard](https://app.zorveus.com). They are separate from organization [Webhooks](/platform/webhooks), which deliver signed HTTP events to your backend server.

---

## Delivery channels

You can configure preferences across two channels:

- **`email`**: Sends alert emails to your account email address.
- **`in_app`**: Shows alerts in your dashboard notification inbox.

---

## Alert events

Turn each event on or off in your account's **Notification settings**:

| Event | When it triggers |
| :--- | :--- |
| `low_balance` | Your organization wallet drops below its low-balance alert threshold. |
| `cap_reached` | A spending cap blocks a request. |
| `payment_credited` | A wallet top-up payment is verified and credited. |
| `payment_failed` | A wallet top-up payment attempt fails. |
| `app_connected` | An OAuth app is authorized on your account or organization. |
| `app_revoked` | A connected OAuth app is disconnected or revoked. |
| `provider_credential_added` | A new BYOK provider credential is added to your organization. |
| `provider_credential_rotated` | An existing BYOK provider credential is rotated. |
| `monthly_summary` | Monthly usage digest for your workspace. |

To automate server-side reactions to billing, cap, or BYOK events, configure [Webhooks](/platform/webhooks) instead.
