# Notification Settings

<ApiEndpoint method="PUT" path="/notification-settings" auth="dashboard_session" />

Configure email and webhook alerts when:
- Wallet balance drops below configured threshold (e.g. `$10.00`).
- Spending cap reaches 80% and 100% of limit.
- New member invitations are accepted.
