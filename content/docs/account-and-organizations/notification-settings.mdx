---
title: Notification settings
description: Configure email alerts for spending cap thresholds, wallet low-balance warnings, and invites.
---

Notification settings belong to the current dashboard user. They do not configure organization outbound webhooks.

## Channels

The supported channels are `email` and `in_app`.

## Events

You can enable or disable these event preferences:

- `low_balance`
- `cap_reached`
- `app_connected` and `app_revoked`
- `payment_credited` and `payment_failed`
- `provider_credential_added` and `provider_credential_rotated`
- `monthly_summary`

To deliver organization events to your own server, configure [outbound webhooks](/platform/webhooks).
